# First Thunder's Oath

Ten weapons for the Meadows, made from what you get off Eikthyr: his antler, flint, birch and hide. Every piece has its own model and a little bit of lightning damage.

I wanted the first biome to have a reason to fight Eikthyr more than once. You can skip this set and go straight to the Black Forest, or farm him a few times and head in better armed. It's a step up from the flint gear but it won't beat bronze.

Made with help from Claude.

## What you get

Everything is crafted at a level 2 workbench. Each piece needs wood, flint or hide and one Hard Antler. Upgrades only cost the basic materials.

- Firstfall - mace, 22 blunt
- Stormfurrow - flint-edged sword, 24 slash
- Oakwake - axe, 26 slash. Chops like the flint axe.
- Altarward - one-handed war pike, 30 pierce (see below)
- Stormcleft - sledge, 26 blunt + 5 pierce, built on Stagbreaker so the swing ends in a burst
- Meadow Reaper - atgeir, 32 pierce
- Dawnring - round shield, 10 block
- Fawnfang - dagger, 8 slash + 8 pierce
- Rainrunner - throwing spear, 26 pierce. It flies with its own model and you pick the same one back up.
- Stillbranch - bow, 26 pierce

Every weapon also does 4 lightning damage, +1 per upgrade level. The pike, sledge and atgeir cost 20% less stamina than normal, since you don't have much stamina that early.

Animations and sounds come from the vanilla weapon each one is based on.

## The war pike

The pike is built on the bone war pike from Therzie's Warfare, so with Warfare installed you get its moveset (one-handed, Spears skill). Without Warfare it still works, it just acts like an atgeir.

## Install

Use a mod manager, or copy the `plugins` folder contents into `BepInEx/plugins/FirstThundersOath`. The `assets` folder has to stay next to the DLL.

Needs Jotunn.

## Multiplayer

It adds items, so everyone on the server needs it, and so does a dedicated server.

## Good to know

- If you uninstall the mod, the items disappear from your inventory and chests. That's how modded items work.
- Spawn names: `MaceFirstThunder`, `SwordFirstThunder`, `AxeFirstThunder`, `PikeFirstThunder`, `SledgeFirstThunder`, `AtgeirFirstThunder`, `ShieldFirstThunder`, `KnifeFirstThunder`, `SpearFirstThunder`, `BowFirstThunder`.
